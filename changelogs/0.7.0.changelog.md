# Stourify 0.7.0

## Lists now refresh when you come back to them

Add a spot, then open **Spots near me** — a screen you had looked at earlier — and it said **"No spots
nearby"**, while a pin for that very spot sat on the map above it.

Nothing was broken and nothing failed. The app keeps a screen you have navigated away from, so
coming back showed you the answer from the last time you opened it. **Discover**, **Spots near me**
and **Saved spots** now ask again when you return to them.

## Swipe through a spot's photos

The picture at the top of a spot showed the first photo and nothing else, so a place with five
photos looked exactly like a place with one.

Swipe it now, with a dot for each photo underneath. Tapping still opens the full-screen view.

## The photo viewer says where you are

Opening a spot's photos gave you a picture and a Back button and nothing else — no way to tell which
place you were looking at, especially if you had arrived from a search or put the phone down for a
minute.

It now shows the spot's name under the Back button, and **"Photo 2 of 5"** underneath that.

## Also fixed

- **Your spots show their photos.** A photo added to a spot was never sent to your phone after the
  first moment, so **My spots** kept showing an empty grey square. Photos added from this version on
  arrive properly.

  Spots you added **before** this version will stay blank — their photos were never sent and there
  is nothing left to trigger it. Get in touch if you have some that matter.

## Notes

- The version at the bottom of the sign-in and Settings screens should read **0.7.0**.
- A spot you save takes up to a couple of minutes to appear under **Saved spots**, because it uploads
  in the background. The heart on the spot itself shows straight away.
- The **Privacy Policy**, **Terms** and **account deletion** pages now open correctly.
