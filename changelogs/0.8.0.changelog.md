# Stourify 0.8.0

## Where a spot is, under its name — and tap it for directions

The address and coordinates used to live inside the **About** tab. A spot opens on **Posts**, so the
one thing everybody wants from a place — *where is it* — was behind a tap nothing invited you to
make. On a spot with no description that tab looked bare enough to seem broken.

It is now under the spot's name, visible whichever tab you are on. **Tap it and your map app opens
at that point**, so you can get directions straight away.

A spot whose owner has hidden its location still shows roughly where it is, and is deliberately not
tappable — there would be nothing to open.

## Notes

- The version at the bottom of the sign-in and Settings screens should read **0.8.0**.
- Everything from **0.7.0** and **0.7.1** is included: lists refresh when you come back to them, a
