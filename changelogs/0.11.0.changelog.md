# Stourify 0.11.0

A new look for the screens you use most, and messages that tell you what actually went wrong.

## A new look for Home, Discover and Activity

The Home feed, a post and its comments, Discover and Activity now follow Stourify's new design:
rounded filter pills, a proper search box, a round back button beside the page title, and a new set
of icons throughout. Nothing was taken away. You see the same posts and spots as before, and they
still work when you're offline.

## Adding a spot is now three short steps

**New Spot** walks you through it: fill in the details, put the pin on the map, then check
everything over before you share it. The red error under a field goes away as soon as you start
fixing that field.

Buttons pinned to the bottom of a screen now stay above the keyboard while you type, instead of
hiding behind it.

## When something goes wrong, the app says what

Many screens used to say "check your connection" whatever the real problem was. Search, the tag
page, Discover, Saved spots, profiles and followers, the spot screens and a single post now tell you
what actually happened, for example that the server was slow to answer, or that what you asked for
is no longer there.

- A post that can't be loaded says so, instead of loading forever.
- If a note on a spot's About tab doesn't post, the app tells you why.
- Someone else's profile no longer claims they never set one up when it simply failed to load.
- A slow answer is no longer reported as a connection problem.

## Fixed

- Labels on tabs and buttons are no longer cut off on some Android phones.
- The photo gallery's Back button no longer sits on top of the clock at the top of the screen.

## Notes

- The version at the bottom of the sign-in and Settings screens should read **0.11.0**.
- Nothing you have saved on the phone is affected by this update, including anything created while
  you were offline and waiting to send.
