# Stourify 0.3.0

Discover is a real screen now — browse spots as photos, or see where they are on a map.

## New
- **Discover shows real places, as a grid of photos.** The tab used to be a placeholder. It now
  opens on a two-column mosaic of spots, and tapping one opens it.
- **A map view, one tap from the grid.** The grid answers "what is there"; the map answers "what is
  near the thing I'm looking at". Tap a pin for a small card, tap the card to open the spot, and
  use recenter to get back.
- **The map opens on something even with location off.** It falls back to your home city, then to
  General Santos. Every step after the first works with the radio off.

## Improved
- The photo grid loads thumbnails rather than full-size uploads, so it fills in over a phone
  connection instead of appearing to hang.
- Yesterday's Discover page renders instantly and refreshes in the background — it stays readable
  with no signal.

## Fixed
- **A spot's photo gallery no longer claims the spot has no photos when it simply couldn't reach
  the server.** It now says the request failed and offers **Try again**, shows a placeholder while
  loading, and keeps photos you already have when a refresh fails.

## Notes
- The filter chips on Discover are still decorative — wiring them up needs a category rule the
  server doesn't have yet, and a chip that silently returns the unfiltered list is worse than one
  that plainly does nothing.
