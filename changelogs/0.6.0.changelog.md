# Stourify 0.6.0

## Spots you add are now actually visible

This is the important one, and it is worth reading even if you skip the rest.

Until this version, every spot you added was saved but never published. It appeared in **My spots**
and nowhere else — not in Discover, not in search, not on the map, not in the nearby list. Nobody
could find it, including you. The app never said anything was wrong, because as far as it knew
nothing was.

Spots you add now go live. If you were wondering why nothing you added ever showed up, that was
why, and it was not you.

**Spots you added on an earlier version are still hidden.** They are not lost. My spots now marks
them **"Draft — not visible to anyone"**, so you can see which ones are affected. Get in touch and
they can be published.

## Also new

- **Saved spots have somewhere to live.** Tapping the heart on a spot always worked; there was
  simply no screen showing you the result. There is now a **Saved spots** button on your profile.
- **Discover's category buttons work.** Nature, Foodie, Coast and the rest actually filter now.
  They never did before — pressing one changed nothing. **Trending** has gone: nothing was ever
  filed under it. **All** replaces it.
- **Reply threads say what they are about.** Opening the replies on a note used to lose the spot
  and the note you were reading. Both are now shown above the conversation.
- **My spots shows your photos** instead of grey rectangles.

## Fixed

- The keyboard no longer covers the box where you write a note about a place.
- The spot page is less crowded — Save moved onto the photo, and the rating line now takes you
  straight to the reviews.
- Searching an empty Stourify no longer tells you to try different words when there is simply
  nothing there yet.

## One known problem

**The Privacy Policy, Terms and Account Deletion links will not open.** They point at
`stourify.com`, which is not yet set up to serve pages securely. This is being fixed. Nothing else
in the app is affected, and the same documents are reachable at `api.stourify.com/privacy`,
`/terms` and `/account-deletion` in the meantime.

## Notes

- The version at the bottom of the sign-in and Settings screens should read **0.6.0**.
- Anything you saved while offline is still on your phone and will send once you are signed in.
