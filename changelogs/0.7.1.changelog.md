# Stourify 0.7.1

A small follow-up to 0.7.0.

## The review pages now say which spot they are about

Going from a spot to its reviews gave you a page headed **"Reviews"** and nothing else — it could
have been the reviews of anywhere. The form for writing one had the same problem.

Both now show the spot's name under the title. **← Back** also moved onto its own line, with the
title underneath it rather than beside it.

## Notes

- The version at the bottom of the sign-in and Settings screens should read **0.7.1**.
- Everything in **0.7.0** is included. If you skipped it: lists refresh when you come back to them,
  a spot's photos can be swiped through, and the photo viewer says which spot you are looking at.
