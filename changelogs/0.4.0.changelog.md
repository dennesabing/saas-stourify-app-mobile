# Stourify 0.4.0

A lot of things that looked like they worked now actually do — and when the app cannot reach the
server it finally says so instead of pretending there is nothing there.

## New
- **Posts you write with no signal send themselves later.** Write it, close the app, get on with
  your day. It goes when you are back in coverage.
- **Drafts.** A post you started but did not share is kept, photo and all, and there is a Drafts
  page to find it days later.
- **A spot's About tab is a noticeboard.** Leave a note about a place, and read and reply to the
  notes other visitors have left.
- **Hashtags are links.** Tap one and you land on a page gathering everything carrying it.
- **Offline & sync tries again when you open it.** Opening that screen used to only report; now it
  also has a go at sending whatever is waiting.

## Improved
- Every password box has a **Show** button, including the one that confirms deleting your account.
- The Offline & sync banner counts a photo that is waiting to upload — it used to say nothing was
  waiting while something was.
- Your Profile tab and Settings work with no signal, so you are not locked out of your own account
  screens on a bad connection.
- Anything you saved offline is still there after the app restarts — every time, not just the
  first time.
- The on-screen keyboard no longer covers the field you are typing into.

## Fixed
- **Liking a post works.** It never has before.
- **A spot's photo grid shows the photos taken there.** It never has before.
- **The two privacy switches in Settings do something.** Neither ever has.
- **The "✓ Verified" tag appears on a verified spot** — for the first time in the app's life.
- Replying to a comment puts your reply in the thread, under the comment it answers, and a comment
  you just posted stays where you left it.
- Re-sending a post's photo can no longer attach it twice, and an interrupted send can no longer
  leave a stray copy behind.
- **Screens stopped claiming there is nothing there when they simply could not ask.** Reviews,
  comments, activity, your posts, your blocked list, follow suggestions, the photo gallery and
  search now tell you the request failed and offer **Try again** — and they keep showing whatever
  they had already loaded.
- Onboarding keeps the interests and home city you picked, and its buttons no longer sit under the
  phone's own navigation bar.

## Notes
- **The app's package name changed** (to `com.zivsluck.stourify`). Android treats that as a
  different app, so if you have an older Stourify installed you will need to remove it before this
  one will install. Your account is on the server — signing back in brings everything with it.
- Every native library in this build is compiled for 16 KB memory pages, which newer Android
  phones require.
- Each screen now shows which build it is, at the bottom of the sign-in and Settings screens. If
  you report a problem, that line is the useful thing to include.
