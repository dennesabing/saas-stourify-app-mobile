# Stourify 0.9.0

## The app stops blaming your connection for something else

If the server turned a request down, the feed used to say it could not reach the server at all —
so you went and checked your wifi, and your wifi was fine. It was the wrong message: the phone had
reached us perfectly well, and we had said no.

The feed now tells you what actually happened, and only says the connection is the problem when the
connection really is the problem.

## A spot's description is its own words again

The description now sits in its own panel on the spot page, so you can see at a glance which part
is the spot speaking and which part is everything else around it.

## Writing a review starts where the reviews are

The **Write a review** button has moved off the spot page and onto that spot's reviews page. It sat
on the spot page competing with everything else there; now it is where you are already reading what
other people said.

## An old copy of the app will tell you it is old

If your installed version is too old to work properly with the service, the app now says so plainly
and points you at the download page — instead of simply failing at things and leaving you to guess
why.

## Notes

- The version at the bottom of the sign-in and Settings screens should read **0.9.0**.
- Nothing you have saved on the phone is affected by this update, including anything created while
  you were offline and waiting to send.
- Some of this release is work you cannot see: the app now keeps a record of why it signed itself
  out, and can be asked to explain a sync that appears to do nothing. Both exist so that a problem
  reported once can actually be found.
